# 🎲 Yatzy

A browser-based Yatzy dice game for 1–4 players on the same screen. No installs, no dependencies: just one `index.html` file.

## Play

**Play online:** https://zakir987.github.io/yatzy/

Or download `index.html` and open it in any browser.

## How to play

- On your turn you get up to **3 rolls**. Between rolls, click a die to **hold** it.
- After rolling, click an empty box in your column to score it (you can score 0).
- The game ends after 15 rounds, and the highest total wins.

| Category | Score |
|---|---|
| Ones – Sixes | Sum of dice showing that number |
| Bonus | 50 if Ones–Sixes total 63 or more |
| One Pair | Sum of the highest pair |
| Two Pairs | Sum of two different pairs |
| Three of a Kind | Sum of the three matching dice |
| Four of a Kind | Sum of the four matching dice |
| Small Straight | 1-2-3-4-5 = 15 |
| Large Straight | 2-3-4-5-6 = 20 |
| Full House | Three of one + two of another = sum of all dice |
| Chance | Sum of all dice |
| Yatzy | Five of a kind = 50 |

Tip: press **Space** to roll.
