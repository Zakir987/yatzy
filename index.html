How to play

On your turn, roll up to 3 times (you can change this). Between rolls, click a die to hold it. Then pick one empty box on the scorecard (you can take 0). 15 rounds, highest total wins.

Ones–Sixes: sum of that number · Upper bonus if Ones–Sixes reach the target · Pair / Two Pairs / 3 & 4 of a Kind: sum of matching dice · Small Straight: any 4 in a row (1-2-3-4, 2-3-4-5 or 3-4-5-6) · Large Straight: any 5 in a row (1-2-3-4-5 or 2-3-4-5-6) · Full House: 3 of one + 2 of another · Chance = sum · Yatzy = 5 of a kind. All points, the bonus, and the straight rules can be changed under Game rules.
