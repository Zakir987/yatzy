🎲 Yatzy

Five dice, three rolls, fifteen boxes.

Number of players
How to play

On your turn, roll up to 3 times. Between rolls, click a die to hold it. Then pick one empty box on your scorecard to score (you can take 0).

Ones–Sixes: sum of that number · Bonus: 50 if upper section ≥ 63 · One Pair / Two Pairs / Three / Four of a Kind: sum of the matching dice · Small Straight 1-2-3-4-5 = 15 · Large Straight 2-3-4-5-6 = 20 · Full House 3 + 2 = sum of all dice · Chance = sum of all dice · Yatzy 5 of a kind = 50.
