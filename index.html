How to play

On your turn, roll up to 3 times. Between rolls, click a die to hold it. Then pick one empty box on the scorecard (you can take 0). 15 rounds, highest total wins.

Ones–Sixes: sum of that number · Bonus: 50 if Ones–Sixes ≥ 63 · Pair / Two Pairs / 3 & 4 of a Kind: sum of matching dice · Small Straight 1-2-3-4-5 = 15 · Large Straight 2-3-4-5-6 = 20 · Full House 3+2 = sum of all dice · Chance = sum · Yatzy = 50.
